# Low Fire (Horizon Native Mod)

A native Horizon mod by **CayMac** that ports the essential **Low Fire** PvP & survival feature to Horizon:

- **Crystal Clear Visibility:** Lowers the intrusive first-person burning fire overlay down to the bottom edge of the screen so your crosshair and view are never obstructed in combat.
- **Customizable Height:**
  - `Low (22%)`: Standard competitive PvP low fire.
  - `Very Low (14%)`: Sleek, minimal flame bar.
  - `Minimal (8%)`: Discreet bottom-border burning indicator.
  - `No Fire (0%)`: Completely hides the first-person fire overlay.
  - `Vanilla (100%)`: Normal vanilla fire rendering.
- **Adjustable Opacity:** Choose between 100%, 75%, and 50% transparency.
- **100% Damage & Server Accurate:** Fire ticks and burning damage logic remain completely unaltered.
- **In-Game Toggle:** Toggleable on the fly with a keybind or in Options > Mods > Low Fire.
